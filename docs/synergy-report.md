## Reading (80 seeds)

- Synergy picker slightly ahead on average (mean 86 vs 81, wins 21 vs 18) but deeper on only 38 of 80 seeds: building on themes and taking good cards at random are close. Note: these 80 seeds include the earlier 40, so repeated pairs aren't fully independent evidence.
- **Designed combos showing up:** Rubber + Ricochet Rush (+22), Lucky Orbs + Critical Mass (+34), the crit cluster (Overcharge / Sharp Eye / Heavier Balls).
- **Unplanned ones:** Momentum + Brutal (+41), Acid + Ricochet Rush (+24), Hazard Suit + area damage (Wide Beam, Copper Wire).
- **Lead Core is a true combo card:** it's weak on its own (43 rows) but sits in the top three runs, both of which reached the 221-turn cap.
- **Physics cards:** Ghost (91) and Wraparound (90) are the best single cards; Magnetic (49) and Lead Core alone (43) are among the weakest.
- **Still weak alone:** Echo, Volatile, Black Powder, Mitosis, Pierce, Tinderbox (46–52 vs a 64 average).
